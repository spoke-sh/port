## Observations

Defining registration as a separate live-state contract kept the change small and avoided mutating the existing static hosted inventory contract before the refresh and placement stories land.

The main friction was tool-generated artifact drift: `keel story record` pointed the second proof annotation at the wrong acceptance criterion, and `keel story reflect` emitted the default TODO scaffold. Both needed manual cleanup before submission.
